Geometry from the DFR0934 datasheet: 41.92 × 27.08 mm glass, 35.05 × 15.32 mm transparent area, 0.274 mm pixel pitch. The bottom 8 controller rows sit on the opaque strip and are not used. The simulation is the firmware's C++ core compiled to WebAssembly, so what you see here is what the ESP32 renders.
